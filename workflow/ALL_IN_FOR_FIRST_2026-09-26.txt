1. THE REAL CONSTRAINTS, AND HOW WE USE UNLIMITED AI AGAINST THEM
=====================================================================
AI tokens are free now. Host slots and verified code are not. So we use AI to get:
 (a) more useful runs per host-hour: working compile cache, no wasted replicates, nothing on
     Trn2 that a CPU can check;
 (b) more host-hours: the 16-chip machine, if Csaba books it;
 (c) bigger wins per run: kernels and Muon batching built in parallel by many agents, raced;
 (d) zero rejected submissions: a dedicated compliance agent.
More agents than independent tasks adds merge conflicts and review load, not speed.
Scale verification with implementation: at least 1 verifier per 3 implementers.
