## Why clock36 changes the confound, but does not prove equivalence

Exact source lines3302–3312 use, for steps0–4,
`max(0, process_age + 30 - 900)` for optimizer schedule progress; from step5
they return existing charged elapsed. Thus first-five samples at process age
120 or845 seconds both yield0; age871 yields1, age1000 yields130. Unlike old
3e79, a sub870-second cold versus warm start need not feed hundreds of seconds
of different optimizer progress into first-five updates. This is source
reasoning, not an executed independent CPU proof or native tensor comparison.

Actual step-start ages differ from observer fifth-boundary times. A boundary
below900 does not alone prove all source samples below870. Later time-based
schedule/stop decisions, compilation specializations and asynchronous timing
still can change steps and trained parameters. Do not pool warm controls with
cold controls merely because the helper zeroes early progress; archive cache
regime and obtain new same-host warm controls before warm candidate comparisons.
No quality gain or cold submission equivalence follows from startup savings.
