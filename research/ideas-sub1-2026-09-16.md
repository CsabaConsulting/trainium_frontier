# Novel, rules-safe ideas for sub-1.0 bpb (2026-09-16)

Hard constraints (from `README.md:211-246`, `prepare.py:596-887`): pure,
stateless, causal forward; fixed BPE ids with logit slice 8192; trusted
`evaluate_bpb` on a pinned private shard; train from scratch on provided data
only; 1800 s on 1 Trn2 chip. Anything needing eval-time state, external data,
pretrained weights, tokenizer changes, or cross-batch memory is banned — those
are excluded below by construction.

## Already ON (don't re-propose)

Frozen byte-n-gram embedding/VE tables + in-loop logit mixer; bf16 tables;
softcap CE 15.0; Muon; EMA-preferring eval. Ideas below stack on top.

## Tier 1 — small, legal, untested to our knowledge

1. **Byte-length loss weighting.** bpb = CE × bytes/token factor, but training
   minimizes mean token CE — frequent short tokens dominate gradients while
   rare long tokens dominate bpb. Weighting token loss by byte length aligns
   the objective with the metric. Read-only, stateless, zero-step-cost.
2. **N-gram order / backoff-shape search.** BYTE_NGRAM=3 is locked without a
   recorded {2,4,5} comparison under the CURRENT stack (EMA + softcap + Muon).
   Higher orders cost table memory but are frozen; smoke-test 2/4/5.
3. **In-loop mixer temperature / weighting schedule.** The logit mixer weight
   is a constant; scheduling it (high early for fast descent, decay late for
   model purity) or cooling it near the end may capture both regimes.
4. **Second frozen table family: skip-grams / gap-grams.** Same legality as
   the current n-gram tables (frozen counts, stateless lookup), different
   signal (e.g. tokens t-2,t with t-1 marginalized). Additive logit term.
5. **Position-aware n-gram backoff.** Current tables are position-blind;
   separate early-position vs late-position counts (2 shards of the same
   frozen data) cost one extra lookup and may fix the seq-2048 boundary
   behavior the ROPE_BASE experiments hinted at.

## Tier 2 — medium bets

6. **Self-distillation within budget.** Train a first ~1200 s model, freeze it,
   distill into a fresh model for the remaining ~600 s — all on provided data,
   final artifact still one static student. Two-phase time split needs tuning,
   but the teacher's soft targets are free extra signal per step.
7. **Curriculum by n-gram surprise.** Order documents by frozen-model loss
   (easy→hard) computed offline once from counts, not from training. Read-only
   input ordering; pairs with P5-reshuffle infra.
8. **Auxiliary byte-prediction head (training only).** Predict next byte alongside
   next token; head discarded before `load_for_eval`. Extra gradient per step,
   zero eval footprint. Must verify it doesn't slow steps/s past its benefit.
9. **Cooldown-shape surgery.** Current cooldown (momentum frac 0.045, flat LR
   assumptions) was tuned jointly with an older stack. A sharper final decay
   (e.g. linear→zero over last 5–10% of wall time rather than steps) directly
   targets final-bpb, and wall-time-based scheduling is robust to step-rate
   variance across runs.

## Tier 3 — outside-the-box (verify legality first, then smoke)

10. **Depth/width re-think for the 30-min token count.** ~280M tokens in-budget
    implies a Chinchilla-optimal dense size far above a 6-layer d640 model —
    but small-data repetition flips the usual prescription (repeated tokens
    favor smaller models + more epochs). The team already walks this line;
    the novel move is to *measure* the repetition curve: bpb vs epochs at
    fixed wall time for 2–3 sizes, fit it, and let the fit pick depth/width
    instead of grid intuition.
11. **Layer-type heterogeneity.** All 6 layers are identical blocks. Alternating
    block types (e.g. wide-MLP layers interleaved with narrow ones, or
    attention-only early layers + MLP-heavy late layers) keeps parameter count
    and step cost flat while adding capacity where the loss needs it.
    Position space in `has_ve()` already assumes heterogeneity for VE layers.
12. **Frozen-count unigram prior as init, not mixer.** Instead of mixing n-gram
    logits at every step (which the model must learn to trust/cancel),
    initialize the unembedding bias with log-unigram and let training deviate.
    Same information, zero per-step interference; frees the mixer to carry
    only higher-order signal.

## Explicitly rejected (for the record)

Eval-time kNN/RETRO/caches (stateful, banned); BPE retraining (SHA gate);
pretrained teachers / external text (data rule); byte-level architectures
(logit-slice rule); multi-chip or over-budget "records"; extra packages.
