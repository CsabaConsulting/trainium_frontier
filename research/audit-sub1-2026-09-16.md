# Sub-1.0 bpb audit — where we stand and what's missing (2026-09-16)

Source: 6-agent workflow sweep over `trainium_frontier/` (starter code, research
plans, ~553 run logs, neighboring Neuron/NKI repos) plus direct spot-checks of
`train.py`, `prepare.py`, and `README.md`. Agent-reported numbers are labeled
[agents]; directly verified facts are labeled [verified].

## 1. Locked configuration (current best)

- [verified] `train.py:201` SEQ_LEN=2048, `:202` DEPTH=6, batch 131072 tokens,
  Muon optimizer (`USE_COMPILE_MUON=True`, NS_STEPS=5), LOGIT_SOFTCAP=15.0.
- [verified] Aids ON: `USE_NGRAM_VE=True` (`:607`), byte n-gram tables in bf16,
  ROPE+NORM on, `EVAL_PREFER_EMA` defaults to 1.
- [agents] Floor (1-chip, in-budget, in-process eval): **1.004355** (seed 42,
  step 2139); rehearsal 1.004371. Seed 42–44 spread ≈ 0.0061 — half the
  remaining gap is seed noise.
- [agents] Throughput wall: ~2135 steps / ~280M tokens per 1800 s budget.
  Raw minimum 0.987716 exists but is **over-budget** (ws=4) — out of scope,
  do not chase it.

## 2. Rules that constrain every idea (read them before proposing anything)

- [verified] `README.md:220`: model must be a **pure, stateless, causal**
  forward — logits a function of input tokens only. `load_for_eval` hook;
  score by trusted `evaluate_bpb()` in `prepare.py:596` on a pinned private
  shard under a time budget.
- Consequence: eval-time caches, kNN/RETRO datastores, cross-batch state,
  BPE/tokenizer swaps (SHA gate), pretrained or external data, touching val,
  extra packages, sub-core tricks, and over-budget records are all banned or
  out of scope. A byte-level (BLT-style) model is effectively banned: fixed
  BPE ids + logit slice 8192.

## 3. Critical gaps — things to revisit, not new ideas

1. **Three eval definitions are being compared as one number.** [agents]
   1.018985 vs 1.004355 vs 1.003045 (sidecar) come from different eval defs.
   Action: score the SAME checkpoint under all three defs, record
   tokens/shards each time, and label every future number. Nothing else is
   interpretable until this is done.
2. **Depth/width closures are stale-regime evidence.** D7/8 and W384–1024
   "closed negative" verdicts date partly from the 5-minute regime that
   favored 4-layer models. DEPTH=8 losing at full 1800 s is recorded in a code
   comment (`train.py:202`), but width has not had a clean full-budget
   resweep since the n-gram + EMA + softcap stack landed. Revisit width once,
   at full budget, with EMA eval.
3. **Seed + EMA variance sets the significance bar.** Spread 0.0061 across 3 seeds
   with EMA already on. A public-best seed hunt was considered and DROPPED
   (2026-09-16): best-of-N on the visible eval is selection bias with ~zero
   expected transfer to the pinned private shard. Keep the spread as a bar
   instead — future improvements must clear ~0.003 to count as signal — and
   fix seed 42 as the control seed for all comparisons.
4. **Throughput IS capacity.** At ~2135 steps the run sees ~280M tokens.
   A 5% step-rate gain (NKI attention forward port) buys ~100 steps, which is
   the same order as the gap. Kernel work and model work are the same lever.
5. **Two flagged candidates were never actually tested:** MXFP8/FP8 pipeline
   (zero hits in the training files — legality and compile cost unknown) and
   GQA (`n_kv_head` exists in `./autoresearch/train.py` but was never ported
   to the Trainium model). Both are timeboxed spikes, not commitments.
6. **Batch/LR interaction is under-swept.** The 65k-batch run (3544 steps,
   1.0488) lost, but batch size, LR, warmup (300), and cooldown fraction were
   never swept jointly at full budget. The warmup=300 optimum itself was
   confirmed by resweep — apply that same discipline to batch×LR once.

## 4. What NOT to revisit

SSM/Mamba-2, RWKV/QRWKV, YAT/SLAY exotic kernels: tried, negative, and each
retest costs a full-budget slot. Keep them closed unless a new combination
(ngram + EMA + softcap) gives a specific reason — and then test only the
combination, at 300-step smoke scale first.
